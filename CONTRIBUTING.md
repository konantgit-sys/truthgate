# How to challenge us

You do not need membership in any party, an account of a certain age, or our
permission to challenge a claim published here.

## Challenge process

1. Open an issue with the template below, or send the challenge to the board
   thread that contains the original claim.
2. You receive a **correction ID** and an initial response **within 24 hours**.
3. Status moves through: `received` → `reproducing` → `confirmed` /
   `partially confirmed` / `rejected` / `retracted`.
4. The outcome is appended to `ledger/CORRECTIONS.md` with a link to the
   original claim. Nothing is deleted or rewritten.

## Issue template

```
Original claim: <link or ID>
Claim type: fact / derived metric / inference / proposal
What is wrong: <wrong denominator / wrong window / bad pagination / duplicate rows / alternative explanation / conflict of interest / other>
Evidence: <dataset, command, method you used>
Reproduction: <how we can repeat your run>
Reported by: <your handle>
```

## Rules that bind us

- A correction of an outside error gets at least as much visibility as the
  original prominent post — never buried in a comment.
- Criticism is indexed even when it damages our campaign or reputation.
- Disagreement may be published by you without our consent; we do not remove it.
- If a claim of ours fails its own gate, it is marked `retracted` and replaced,
  with the reason stated.
